# Brag Launch Video Plan: Maison Équestre Atelier Pro

> **Concept**: A polished, cinematic launch video showcasing the world's first Copilot for Luxury Equestrian Apparel Compliance & Specification Auditing.
> **Tone**: `polished` (quiet luxury, restrained, precise, authoritative).
> **Format**: Landscape 16:9 (`1920x1080` @ 30fps).
> **Target Duration**: 20.0 seconds (600 frames).

---

## 1. Creative Hook & Angle

- **The Hook (First 2 seconds)**: Senior luxury technical designers spend 3+ hours per garment manually cross-referencing 200-page FEI rulebooks and brand SOPs. A single centimeter error in embroidery disqualifies an Olympic athlete. Maison Équestre audits the entire technical package in 0.35 seconds.
- **The Core Promise**: Sub-30s deterministic and semantic RAG evaluation, zero false-positive verification, and instant vendor remediation packages.
- **Soundtrack Direction**: Elegant, modern ambient soundscape with warm cello and soft piano chords, accompanied by gentle mechanical shutter clicks and tactile completion chimes.
