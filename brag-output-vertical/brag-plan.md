# Vertical Brag Launch Video Plan: Maison Équestre Atelier Pro

> **Concept**: A vertical, mobile-first cinematic launch video (9:16 portrait) showcasing the world's first Copilot for Luxury Olympic Equestrian Apparel Compliance & Specification Auditing.
> **Format**: Vertical (`1080x1920` @ 30fps), 9:16 aspect ratio tailored for Reels, TikTok, and Shorts.
> **Tone**: `polished` (quiet luxury, editorial, authoritative, calm).
> **Target Duration**: 20.0 seconds (600 frames).

---

## 1. Creative Hook & Angle

- **The Hook (First 2.5s)**: In Olympic equestrian jumping and dressage, apparel is safety and sporting equipment. A 1cm embroidery error disqualifies an athlete. Designers spend 3+ hours per garment manually reviewing 200-page rulebooks. Maison Équestre audits technical packages in 0.35 seconds.
- **Visual Composition**: Full vertical phone layout. Clean cards, generous breathing room, deep equestrian forest background (`#0C1F16`), warm brass gold accents (`#C5A880`), and sharp typography.
- **Soundtrack Direction**: Elegant ambient soundscape with gentle acoustic piano chords, warm cello drone, and mechanical shutter clicks on transitions.
