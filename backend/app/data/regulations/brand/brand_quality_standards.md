# Luxury Equestrian Brand - Technical & Quality Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

## Metadata
- **Organization**: Maison Équestre Luxury Group
- **Standard**: Technical Apparel Compliance & Production SOP
- **Effective Version**: 2026.1
- **Applicability**: Competition Jackets, Tailcoats, Technical Shirts, Breeches

---

### Section 1: Financial & Costing Benchmarks (FOB/COGS)
- **BRAND-SOP-COGS-01 (Show Jackets)**: Maximum Target FOB is $55.00 USD. Hard ceiling is $65.00 USD. Any tech pack with actual FOB exceeding $65.00 USD is marked as a critical financial violation.
- **BRAND-SOP-COGS-02 (Tailcoats)**: Maximum Target FOB is $85.00 USD. Hard ceiling is $95.00 USD.
- **BRAND-SOP-COGS-03 (Technical Shirts)**: Maximum Target FOB is $22.00 USD. Hard ceiling is $28.00 USD.

---

### Section 2: Technical Fabric & Material Performance Standards
- **BRAND-SOP-FABRIC-01 (Breathability)**: All primary performance fabrics for competition show jackets and tailcoats must achieve a water vapor transmission rate (breathability) of at least 10,000 g/m²/24h (JIS L 1099 B-1 or ASTM E96). Any fabric below 10,000 g/m²/24h shall be flagged with a performance WARNING.
- **BRAND-SOP-FABRIC-02 (4-Way Stretch)**: Outer shell fabrics must have a minimum of 15% elastane/spandex blend or achieve at least 18% mechanical stretch in both warp and weft to ensure rider shoulder articulation over fences.
- **BRAND-SOP-FABRIC-03 (Sun Protection)**: All outdoor competition fabrics must possess a certified UPF rating of at least UPF 50+.
- **BRAND-SOP-FABRIC-04 (Weight/GSM)**: Summer competition jackets must not exceed 280 GSM. All-season jackets must range between 290 GSM and 360 GSM.

---

### Section 3: Garment Tailoring, Tolerances, and Construction
- **BRAND-SOP-TOL-01 (Collar Height Variance)**: Production tolerance for collar stand height must not exceed +/- 0.5 cm from the graded specification.
- **BRAND-SOP-TOL-02 (Chest & Waist Variance)**: Half-chest and half-waist measurement tolerance must not exceed +/- 1.0 cm.
- **BRAND-SOP-TOL-03 (Sleeve Length Variance)**: Total sleeve length from shoulder seam to cuff must not exceed +/- 0.75 cm.
- **BRAND-SOP-SEAM-01 (Stitch Density)**: Top-stitching and lapel seams must maintain 12 to 14 stitches per inch (SPI). Seams below 10 SPI are rejected for structural durability.

---

### Section 4: Brand Identity & Aesthetics
- **BRAND-SOP-LOGO-01 (Elegance Threshold)**: Brand logos must be tonal or discreet metallic embroidery. High-contrast rubber or neon PVC patches are forbidden on luxury competition apparel.
- **BRAND-SOP-PIPING-01 (Piping Width)**: Contrast piping on lapels or collar must not exceed 3.5 mm width to maintain subtle understated luxury.
