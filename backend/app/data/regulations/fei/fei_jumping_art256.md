# FEI Jumping Rules - Article 256: Dress, Protective Headgear, and Commercial Identification

## Metadata
- **Federation**: Fédération Equestre Internationale (FEI)
- **Discipline**: Show Jumping
- **Rulebook**: FEI Jumping Rules
- **Article**: Article 256
- **Category**: Dress & Identification

---

### Article 256.1: Dress Regulations for Athletes
1.1. In all competitions, Athletes must be properly turned out.
1.2. A protective headgear must be worn at all times while mounted.
1.3. A riding jacket is mandatory for all international jumping competitions, unless the Ground Jury grants permission to ride without a jacket due to extreme heat or weather conditions.
1.4. Jackets may be of any color, provided they present a neat, dignified appearance.
1.5. Contrast collars and contrast lapels are permitted. Contrast piping around the collar, lapels, and pockets is permitted.
1.6. Competition shirts must have a collar. Shirts may be long or short-sleeved. Ties or hunting stocks are required for men; women may wear shirts with a stand-up collar.

---

### Article 256.3: Commercial Identification, Brand Emblems, and Logos on Apparel
3.1. **Collar Logo**: While present in the competition arena and prize-giving ceremonies, the name and/or logo of the Athlete's sponsor, or the garment manufacturer's brand emblem, may appear on the collar of the jacket. The total surface area of this identification must not exceed sixty square centimeters (60 cm²).
3.2. **Chest Logo**: A sponsor or manufacturer logo may appear on the chest/pocket area of the competition jacket on one side only. The total surface area of this logo must not exceed two hundred square centimeters (200 cm²).
3.3. **Sleeve Identification**: A brand or sponsor logo may appear on each sleeve of the competition jacket, with each logo not exceeding one hundred square centimeters (100 cm²).
3.4. **Collar Lettering**: Alternatively, one line of lettering may appear vertically along the collar edge, provided the letters do not exceed three centimeters (3 cm) in height and twelve centimeters (12 cm) in total length.
3.5. **National Flag / NF Badge**: The national flag of the Athlete's National Federation or NF country code (three-letter code) may appear on the chest or upper arm with a maximum area of one hundred square centimeters (100 cm²).
3.6. Any garment exceeding these dimensions or displaying unauthorized promotional slogans is strictly non-compliant and shall lead to warning or disqualification under Ground Jury discretion.
