# FEI Eventing Rules - Article 538: Dress and Athlete Protective Equipment

## Metadata
- **Federation**: Fédération Equestre Internationale (FEI)
- **Discipline**: Eventing
- **Rulebook**: FEI Eventing Rules
- **Article**: Article 538
- **Category**: Dress & Safety Equipment

---

### Article 538.1: Dress by Phase
1.1. **Dressage Phase**: The dress regulations follow Article 427 of the FEI Dressage Rules. Tailcoat or competition jacket is permitted.
1.2. **Cross-Country Phase**:
  - A body protector / protective safety vest certified to EN 13158 or ASTM F1937 / BETA Level 3 standard is strictly mandatory.
  - Light technical shirts with long or short sleeves are mandatory. Sleeveless apparel is prohibited for safety reasons.
  - Inflatable airbag vests may only be worn when paired over an approved BETA Level 3 standard protector.
1.3. **Jumping Phase**: Follows Article 256 of the FEI Jumping Rules. Standard show jumping jackets are mandatory.

---

### Article 538.2: Commercial Identification and Identification Area Limits
2.1. Across all phases, manufacturer logos on competition jackets or protective vests must not exceed eighty square centimeters (80 cm²).
2.2. Athlete sponsor logos on the cross-country jersey/shirt front or sleeves must not exceed two hundred square centimeters (200 cm²).
