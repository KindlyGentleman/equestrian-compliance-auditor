# FEI Dressage Rules - Article 427: Dress and Protective Headgear

## Metadata
- **Federation**: Fédération Equestre Internationale (FEI)
- **Discipline**: Dressage

- **Article**: Article 427
- **Category**: Dress & Identification

---

### Article 427.1: Riding Jackets and Tailcoats
1.1. In all CDI competitions, a civilian or military tailcoat (frac) or jacket must be worn.
1.2. **Color Palette**: Tailcoats and jackets may be any dark, conservative color (for example: black, navy blue, midnight blue, dark green, dark brown, or dark grey). Very bright or iridescent colors are not permitted.
1.3. **Collars and Lapels**: Contrast collars and contrast lapels in velvet, satin, or matching fabric are permitted, provided they complement the overall jacket color and remain in conservative tones. Extreme neon colors or distracting multi-color patterns are strictly prohibited.
1.4. **Piping**: Subtle contrast piping along the collar, lapels, and pockets is permitted, provided the piping width does not exceed four millimeters (4 mm).
1.5. **Tailcoat Details**: Tail weights must remain within standard tailoring bounds to maintain clean silhouette in motion. Cutaway points and decorative buttons must be securely attached.
1.6. **Buttons**: Metal buttons (gold, silver, matte brass) or covered fabric buttons are permitted. Embossed monogram crests are permitted provided they are restrained.

---

### Article 427.2: Competition Shirts and Neckwear
2.1. Competition shirts must have a white, cream, or light-colored stand-up collar.
2.2. A white stock tie (plastron) or necktie is required unless the shirt has an integrated decorative stand-up collar with covered closure.
2.3. Shirts must have sleeves. Short sleeves or long sleeves are permitted. Sleeveless shirts are strictly prohibited in the competition arena even when riding without a jacket.

---

### Article 427.3: Commercial Identification & Logos
3.1. Brand manufacturer identification on jackets is restricted to one single emblem on the collar of maximum sixty square centimeters (60 cm²).
3.2. Sponsor logo on the chest is limited to maximum two hundred square centimeters (200 cm²).
3.3. Logos must be discreet and not interfere with the solemn elegance of the dressage presentation.
